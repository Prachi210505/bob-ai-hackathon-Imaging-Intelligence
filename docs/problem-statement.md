# Problem Statement

## Background

Clinical trials collect safety and efficacy evidence across many sites and thousands of patient visits. Each site must follow a protocol that defines visit windows, treatment doses, prohibited medicines, assessments, and data-handling requirements.

## The Problem

Risk managers often discover protocol deviations after manual reconciliation, late monitoring, or an FDA audit. Missed visits, incorrect doses, prohibited co-medications, and incomplete assessments can compromise subject safety and data integrity before a central team knows which site needs help.

## Who Is Affected

The primary user is a clinical trial risk manager or clinical operations lead responsible for comparing site performance, triaging deviations, and assigning corrective and preventive actions. Site monitors and medical monitors are secondary users who need the evidence behind each escalation.

## Why It Matters

For a large trial, a rejected or delayed submission can add 6–12 months to the approval timeline and cost tens of millions of dollars. The immediate cost is also operational: teams spend time searching across patient, visit, medication, and site records instead of investigating the highest-risk signals first.

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Existing workflows combine EDC exports, spreadsheets, periodic monitoring, and manual review. Those tools can store the records, but they do not consistently compare every record to the current protocol or show a transparent site-level priority with linked evidence. TrialGuard makes that comparison repeatable and reviewable.
